Data-Efficient Post-Training for Multimodal Foundation Models

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at UC Davis, advised by Prof. Lifu Huang. My research focuses on data-efficient post-training of multimodal foundation models.

I study data selection, data synthesis, and data-centric analysis for post-training and continual pre-training to improve long-horizon planning and multimodal reasoning in VLMs and LLMs.

01 / Research

Research Interests

My primary research interests are in Natural Language Processing and Multimodal Learning, with a focus on Data-Efficient Post-Training of (Multimodal) Large Language Models.

01

Data Selection & Synthesis

Selecting and synthesizing targeted training data from model errors and capability gaps for data-efficient LMM post-training.

  • Data selection
  • Data synthesis
  • LMM post-training
  • Continual pre-training
02

Multimodal Learning

Efficiently adapting large multimodal models to emerging problems and diverse downstream tasks.

  • Vision-language models
  • Multimodal reasoning
  • Grounding
03

Agentic Long-horizon Planning

Building agentic models that plan, reason, and adapt across diverse long-horizon tasks and environments.

  • Agentic AI
  • Multimodal planning
  • Long-horizon reasoning
